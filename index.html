Frequently Asked Questions

What it is, why it helps, and why it stands out.

What is it?

BloodNet is a donor network that connects hospitals, donors, and blood banks in one place.

Why is it useful?

It saves time, finds the right donor faster, and speeds up the response during emergencies.

Why is it better than others?

It matches by priority, distance, and availability, so the search is not random.

Is it easy for hospitals?

Yes, hospitals can raise a request in a few clicks and notify matched donors immediately.

Does it really help?

Yes, faster matching, a clear process, and live notifications improve the chance of help arriving on time.

Why it works

Designed to reduce delay at every step.

BloodNet combines compatibility rules, ranked recommendations, and a clear response workflow so teams spend less time searching and more time acting.
